Your account and privacy

Local profiles, settings, files and Account Center entries are stored in this browser. Google sign-in is required for online access and does not upload your desktop files or synchronize them across devices.

Google sign-in

When you choose Google sign-in, Google shares your basic profile identifier, name and verified email with NebulaHub to identify your account. NebulaHub does not request access to Gmail, Drive or your Google password. A server session lasts up to 24 hours. Signing out revokes it. Expired server session records are removed by scheduled cleanup within three days of creation. The linked profile remains in your local browser until removed.

Account exports

A .hub export can contain saved Account Center entries and a linked Google profile reference, including your name and email. Treat this file as personal data. It contains no Google session, cookies, password or tokens. You must sign in with Google again after importing it into another browser.

The Community Update: usage reports

Basic reporting is enabled for new profiles. Existing opt-outs are preserved, and Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control disables reporting. You can disable it in Google & Privacy. When enabled, NebulaHub records desktop visits and the IDs of supported apps and games opened, with a timestamp and a temporary random session identifier. It does not include searches, messages, private files, passwords, keystrokes or your email address. The developer can see recent activity and usage counts. These records are removed within eight days. Disabling sharing stops new collection; existing records expire normally.

Accounts and access restrictions

Verified Google names, email addresses, account identifiers, first-seen and last sign-in times are stored separately from usage reports. Only the verified site owner can view this directory and suspend, ban or restore accounts. Moderation reasons and actions are stored for accountability. These records remain until removed by the owner; contact the address below for data requests. Suspensions expire at the displayed time. Bans remain until restored. Restrictions apply to online access on this site; previously downloaded files and third-party services cannot be remotely revoked.

Cloudflare Web Analytics

When reporting is enabled, Cloudflare receives page-view and performance measurements. It provides aggregate analytics without cookies or individual visitor tracking. It does not receive your Google profile from NebulaHub. Disabling reports reloads the desktop to stop its loaded script; save work first. Cloudflare controls its analytics retention and infrastructure processing. See Cloudflare Web Analytics.

Hosting and external services

Netlify hosts the site, account sessions, announcements and optional activity records. Hosting providers may process ordinary request metadata such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the service. Games, Google, embedded media, AI and proxy services have their own privacy practices. Opening them can send information to their providers.

Controls and contact
